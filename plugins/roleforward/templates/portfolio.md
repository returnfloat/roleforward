## How to Read This Record

**Source (on every number):**
- **Verified:** confirmed against a system of record.
- **Doc-sourced:** from a named document.
- **Self-reported:** from <Name>'s own account, not checked against a system.
- **Relayed:** secondhand.

**Scope (what a number represents):**
- **Personal:** <Name>'s own output.
- **Team-led:** delivered by a team <Name> led.
- **Program-level:** an organization-wide outcome <Name> contributed to, never presented as personal output.

**Resume-line tags (for building variants):**
- **Track:** `[IC]`, `[LEAD]`, or `[BOTH]`.
- **Area:** `[<area>]`, one per impact area below.
- **Tier:** `[core]` (usually included) or `[opt]` (only when the target role centers that area).

**Only numbers inside a resume line are cleared for external use.** Numbers that appear only in evidence or outcomes are informational.

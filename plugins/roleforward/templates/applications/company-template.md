### Evidence

- **Money:** ownership, latest funding or market cap, profitability or runway, with sources, and what that means for several years of stability.
- **Layoffs and reorgs:** the last two years, sizes, which functions were hit, and whether the function the role sits in was touched.
- **Function leadership:** who leads the function the role sits in, and their tenure, who this role reports to, and what that line says about the function's weight.
- **Function maturity:** public signs of how mature the function is (certifications, published standards, talks or blogs, team size), and what they add up to.
- **Press and incidents:** lawsuits, regulator actions, breaches, recalls, or scandals that touch the function, and how they were handled.
- **Regulation pull:** the rules or contracts that keep the function funded, and how directly they apply to this role.
- **Why the role is open:** posting age and reposts, new vs. backfill wording, the predecessor, other openings on the team, and the read, marked (inferred).

**Red flags:** short phrases, separated by " · "
**Ask the recruiter:** short questions, separated by " · "

### Evidence

Two to four sentences per area, in the table's order:
- **Business and trajectory:** what they sell, revenue and growth, profit or burn.
- **Moat:** narrow, moderate, or wide, and why.
- **Threats:** competitors, AI disruption, regulation, parent or investor priorities.
- **Leadership stability:** CEO, CFO, and CTO tenure and recent exits.
- **Work policy:** remote and RTO history.
- **Employee sentiment:** themes about the function and its working conditions (hearsay).
- **Function governance:** how the function reports and is overseen (for example to the board, a regulator, or an executive), from filings or public material.
- **Team setup:** where the team sits, its charter, size and state, and whether the role is new or a backfill.

**Questions for interviews:** short questions, separated by " · "
